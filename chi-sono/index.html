About me

Guglielmo Rotunno

Computer Engineering student at Politecnico di Milano, in third year.

GitHub ↗LinkedIn ↗The CV as PDFRight nowResearch at NECSTLabWork at SMCPoliMi Data ScientistsLeadTheFuture

Hi! I'm Guglielmo and I study Computer Engineering at Politecnico di Milano. I am from the province of Ravenna, where I attended a science high school in Lugo, and I have lived in Milan since September 2024. My interests lie in applied mathematics: modelling, simulation and numerical computing. I am also interested in artificial intelligence, which I see as a tool in service of these fields.

Since September 2026 I've been doing research at NECSTLab, a research lab at the Politecnico working on computing systems, on federated learning with tree-based models, that is, training a model without moving the data. It's part of LEONARDO, the lab's extracurricular programme, which I've been part of since March 2026. I'm also a consultant at SMC, a company that has worked in heat exchange and fluid storage since 1995: I'm developing its new management software, the one it uses for quotes, orders, delivery notes and invoices.

At PoliMi Data Scientists, the Politecnico's student association for data science and AI, I started as a web developer in October 2025. Then I joined MARCS, a research project of the association to recognise ships that switch off their transponder, where I work on the route model. Today I'm Head of Projects: I coordinate the student teams of the Projects section and, as Team Project Manager, the team behind Clinical Risk Detector for Physiotherapy. I'm also a mentee at LeadTheFuture, a mentorship programme for STEM students.

Outside of coursework I spend a lot of time coding on my own: tools I need, ideas I want to try, and occasionally something optimised more than it needed to be. A few things that came out of it: a program that hunts for bargains on Vinted, a Monte Carlo simulation that estimates the probability that a poker hand wins, and a week of meals computed as an optimisation problem. They're all on the projects page. My course notes and the tools I use to organise university are shared here on the site, for anyone who wants to take and adapt them.

Non-tech side: music production, guitar, piano, chess and bouldering.
