Physics Simulation

Four physics simulations of collisions, animated in Python.

Role On my own·finished

Physics Simulation is a program that animates four mechanics phenomena: the elastic collision, the inelastic collision, the "real" collision with a coefficient of restitution (a number between 0 and 1 that says how much the bodies bounce off each other) and an explosion into fragments, with gravity. You choose masses, velocities and coefficient and watch what happens. It's a small exploration: I wanted to learn object-oriented programming with something I could see moving, and for that I took the physics of the first year of Computer Engineering. It's written in Python and uses Tkinter, the graphics library already included in Python, so there's nothing to install; the code is on GitHub.

The collision formulas

Two quantities matter in the formulas: momentum, that is mass times velocity, summed over the two bodies, which is always conserved in a collision; and kinetic energy, the energy of motion.

The explosion, the fourth simulation, has no collision formula: the last chapter of the animation further down tells its story.
